# Changes version 0.4.2

### Fixes
- On some computers, such as Windows 8 with .NET 4.8, the player often failed to start with the error "Common Language Runtime detected an invalid program". It now starts.
- The player no longer closes by itself when a file is renamed or moved on a drive whose folder path is longer than 260 characters.
- The player no longer becomes unresponsive with menus and keys not working after a dialog disappears without closing; it now recovers automatically within a second.

### Explorer Mode
- Adding a folder to a playlist with Ctrl and a number, or taking it out, now happens at once, even for a large folder. Before, a folder with thousands of files could take several seconds.

### Music
- Holding PageDown or PageUp is lighter on the player: the menu is no longer rebuilt for every track. It is brought up to date once you stop, and the menu opens as quickly as before.

# Changes version 0.4.1

### Window Title
- Choose what the window title shows: press Ctrl+Alt+P, or choose Title Settings in the Settings menu. Each mode has its own choice. The program name is always shown. You can add the mode's name and the names of what is playing: the book, volume and track; the episode and podcast; the track and playlist; or, in Explorer Mode, the file, slot and playlist. Check the items you want and put them in any order with the Move Up and Move Down buttons, or with Alt+Up and Alt+Down.
- A check box leaves out the mode's name while something is loaded.
- The title now changes silently, so your screen reader no longer reads it at every new track. "Read the title aloud" sets, for each mode, when it is still read: when the mode changes (the default), when the mode or what is open in it changes, at every change, or never.
- The hint about Ctrl+F1 is on the title in every mode until you first add a book, podcast or music, or play something in Explorer Mode.

### Explorer Mode
- Links to Windows Media servers, which start with mms://, play.
- If a server's security certificate has expired or is not valid, the player now tells you so and asks whether to connect anyway. Before, the link only failed. Check "Always trust this server" to be asked no more. Press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A to see the servers you trust and remove them. The same applies when you add a podcast or download an episode.
- A link whose server stops sending no longer hangs the player. Playback pauses, the player tells you what went wrong, and Space opens the link again from where it stopped.
- If a slot's file is deleted, the slot takes the next file of its list, paused at its beginning. This works whether the file was deleted while the player was open or while it was closed. If the slot's file is renamed, the slot keeps it under its new name.

### Music
- Choosing a large music folder with Ctrl+D no longer freezes the player. A window shows the folder being read and the files found, and Escape stops the scan without changing your library.
- Large libraries no longer make the player hang on shuffle or next track.
- Changing tracks is quicker, and holding PageDown or PageUp no longer piles up track changes: the last one wins.
- Leaving tracks out of shuffle works with folders as you would expect:
  - Bring back one subfolder or track of a folder you left out, and the rest of the folder stays out.
  - The player says the name of what it left out or brought back.
  - Exclude from Shuffle and Include in Shuffle are in the context menu of tracks and folders.
- In the list of what is left out of shuffle (Ctrl+Shift+S):
  - Ctrl+S brings an item back, and Delete brings everything back after a question.
  - Enter on a folder shows only what is left out inside it, and Backspace returns to the list.
- With shuffle on, a track you removed from the library no longer comes back with Next or Previous.
- A track shorter than the crossfade no longer stops playback when it ends.

### Everywhere
- In a question with Yes and No, press Y or N, or Alt+Y or Alt+N, to answer.
- Questions and messages now use the player's own message box, so NVDA no longer says "unavailable" before reading them.
- The information about a new version opens in a window of its own, instead of replacing what was in the main window.
- Choosing a track in a list no longer announces it again: you already heard it in the list.

# Changes version 0.4

### Explorer Mode (New)
- A new fourth mode, Explorer Mode (F4), plays any audio file on your computer without adding it to your library. Whatever is playing in Book, Podcast, or Music Mode keeps playing in the background while you use it.
- Explorer Mode has ten playback slots. Each slot holds its own file and remembers where you stopped, together with its own volume, speed, equalizer, output device, and other settings. Several slots can play at the same time. When you come back to Explorer Mode, the files of the slots are ready, so the first Space starts at once.
- Press the number keys 1 to 9 and 0 to choose a slot, Ctrl+Tab to move to the next slot that has a file, and Alt with a number to empty a slot. Ctrl+L shows all your slots.
- Give a slot a name of your own with F2 in the slot list, and move a named slot up or down with Alt+Up and Alt+Down. The context menu of the slot list offers everything you can do with a slot.
- The master slot (=) plays nothing: it holds the settings you want your slots to have. Change the volume, speed, equalizer, or any other setting while it is active. Ctrl+Alt with a number gives that slot the master slot's settings, and Ctrl+Alt+= gives them to all slots. Ctrl+Shift with a number, or Ctrl+Shift+= for the master slot, takes the settings back to the program's defaults. Alt+= empties all slots. In the Explorer tab of Ctrl+Y you can choose whether slots that have the master slot's settings change together with it.
- Press Ctrl+T to browse your computer. Only folders that contain audio files are shown, and hidden and system folders are shown or hidden just as Windows Explorer does. When you play a file, the other files in its folder come along, so you can move to the next or previous one just like in Music Mode. Shift+Enter loads a file without playing it.
- Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown move to the previous or next folder, going through subfolders as Music Mode does. With Ctrl+Shift+N, a slot can go on into the next folder by itself at the end of a folder, with PageDown and PageUp, or both. Ctrl+Shift+F chooses when the name of a new folder is read.
- The keys and settings you know from Music Mode work on the slot you are using, including shuffle, repeat, auto-play, the playback queue, the sleep timer, and smooth transitions between songs. M tells you the file and the slot, and N the file that plays next.
- Edit the tags of a file or a whole folder with Ctrl+D, as in Music Mode.
- Press Ctrl+O to search for audio files anywhere on your computer. The results come from the player's own index, so they appear at once.
- Explorer Mode has playlists of its own: press Ctrl+K to create and manage them.
- The context menus of Explorer Mode list everything the keys do, each with its key.
- Listen to internet radio and other online streams: press Ctrl+A and type or paste the address. Playlist files for radio stations (m3u and pls) and HLS streams work too, from a file or from a link, whatever the link's name. The player tells you the name of the station and the song that is playing.
- Pause a live stream, or go back in it, and go forward again up to the live moment. The player keeps up to an hour by default; choose how long in the Explorer tab of Ctrl+Y. F and E tell you how much was received and how far behind live you are.
- A link to an audio file plays like a podcast episode: you can move anywhere in it.
- In a list of many streams, Shift+T lists them all. If the list sorts its channels into groups, as IPTV lists do, Shift+P shows them as a tree of groups, and Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown move to the previous or next group.
- The streams you played recently are listed with Ctrl+Shift+A, and you can save your favorite streams under a name of your choice with Ctrl+Alt+A. Ctrl+C copies a stream's link.
- The Explorer tab of Ctrl+Y sets which slot is active when you start, what happens to the other slots when you open a file, and what happens to the playback of the other modes when you come to Explorer Mode or leave it.

### Open Audio Files from Windows
- Blue Wave Player can now be your default player for audio files. Choose "Register as Default Player" in the Program menu, choose the file types you want, then choose Blue Wave Player in the Windows default apps settings that open.
- A file you open from Windows plays in Explorer Mode. You choose the slot it goes to in the Explorer tab of Ctrl+Y: the first empty slot, the active slot, a slot of your choice, or the slot after the last one used. You also choose what happens when that slot is playing, or when the file is already in a slot. When you open several files together, they play one after another.
- If the player is already open, the file opens there instead of in a second window.

### Music
- Add single audio files from anywhere on your computer with Ctrl+Shift+A. They appear together in a folder named Singles, and the files stay where they are.
- Shuffle is much smarter. In the new Shuffle tab of the Music Playback Settings (Ctrl+Y), you can play every song once before any song repeats, keep songs from the same folder or the same artist apart, and give more chances to songs you have not heard for a while.
- Leave a song or a whole folder out of shuffle with Ctrl+S. Press Ctrl+Shift+S to see everything you left out and bring things back.
- The player now remembers how often each song was played and skipped. Press Ctrl+I on a song or a folder to hear it, and Ctrl+R to start counting again.
- See your most played songs with Ctrl+Alt+L, and the songs you added recently with Ctrl+Shift+Alt+L.

### Audiobooks
- Skip the same introduction or closing music in every track of a book: press Ctrl+Shift+Y and choose how many seconds to skip at the start and at the end of each track. Every book keeps its own setting.
- Delete a book from your computer with Ctrl+Shift+Delete. The player asks before deleting.
- The T and G keys have swapped places: T now tells you how much of the book you have listened to, and G its total length.

### Podcasts
- Look at the episodes of a podcast before you subscribe: in the search results, open its menu and choose Preview Episodes.
- Share your podcasts with a friend, or bring them over from another podcast app: Ctrl+Shift+E saves your podcast list to a file, and Ctrl+Alt+E opens one. You choose which podcasts to include, and whether to keep your categories and the names you gave your podcasts.
- When your connection is slow, an episode you play online pauses to fill up, then plays on without breaks instead of stopping again and again. Choose how long it should play without a break in the Podcast Playback Settings.
- Online episodes start sooner and use less data: each link is asked only once, and links that pass through several redirects no longer keep you waiting.

### Bookmarks
- Jump to the next or previous bookmark with Shift+PageDown and Shift+PageUp.
- Choose when the name of a bookmark is read aloud with Ctrl+Shift+M. The same choice is in the Speech menu.
- Previous Bookmark no longer gets stuck on a bookmark you passed a moment ago: it goes on to the one before it.

### Categories & Playlists
- In the main window, Shift with a number now opens the category or playlist that has that number, and Ctrl with the number adds what you are listening to, or takes it out again.
- An empty list of categories or playlists now tells you so, and that Ctrl+N creates one.

### More Options
- Seek across tracks: when you turn it on, moving past the end or the beginning of a track goes on into the next or previous one. You will find it in the Pause and Seek Fades settings (Ctrl+Shift+I).
- The announcements for track, folder, and book volume changes now have four choices: Disabled, Automatic, Manual, or Automatic and manual.
- Press Ctrl+Shift+P to hear how much internet data the player has used since you opened it.
- Many more audio formats play: WebM and Matroska (many files downloaded from YouTube are in these formats), AC3, ALAC, DSD, Musepack, OptimFROG, Speex, TTA, and audio CD tracks. In Explorer Mode, the sound of video files plays too.
- When a file cannot be played, the player now tells you.
- Alt+F4 now silences the player and closes its window at once; your places and settings are still saved.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed the player sometimes not opening, or opening without your books and settings, when it was started from certain shortcuts.
- If something goes wrong while the player starts, it now tells you what happened instead of closing without a word.
- Opening the player again right after closing it now works.
- Downloaded podcast episodes no longer lose their "downloaded" mark when two episodes of a podcast have the same title.
- Two downloads with the same file name no longer overwrite each other.
- New books no longer disappear from the list for a few minutes while their files are still being copied.
- Castbox links now add the right podcast, and more kinds of Castbox links are recognized.
- Seeking in an online episode past the part that was downloaded, or resuming one at the place you stopped, no longer ends it at once.
- Links on servers that require a modern secure connection now work, and secure sites work through a SOCKS5 proxy too.
- Some M4A files that played silently or not at all now play.
- Folders that Windows marks as system folders, such as a folder with its own icon or one copied from another computer, are no longer left out: the program's folder chooser shows them, and their audio files come into your music library and your books, as Windows Explorer shows them.
- Typing letters to find an item in a list now starts over in a new folder or list, instead of adding to the letters you typed before.
- The Music Library Explorer (Ctrl+T) no longer opens when there is no music folder.
- The second output delay is now only shown when a second output device is chosen.

# Changes version 0.3.1

### Podcast Search
- Search now finds many more podcasts, and finds them even when a name is spaced or written differently: "6minutes", "6 minutes" and "6 Minute" all find the same shows, and "thisamericanlife" finds This American Life.
- Results are now sorted by best match, so the podcast you searched for comes first.
- Choose how results are sorted from the Sort submenu in a result's context menu: Best Match, Last Published Date, Episode Count, or Name. Your choice is remembered for your next searches.
- If a search takes a while, you now hear "Still searching" every few seconds until the results arrive.

### Bug Fixes
- A book now continues exactly where you left it after switching to Podcast or Music Mode and back. Before, it could start its track or chapter again from the beginning.
- After closing the update window opened from Check for Update, keyboard shortcuts work again.
- Podcasts whose feed is missing some details, or has no episodes yet, can now be subscribed to and updated normally, and keep the details shown in the search results.

# Changes version 0.3

Blue Wave Player 0.3 is the first official release.

### Second Audio Output
- Play the same audio on two devices at the same time, for example speakers and headphones together. Choose the second device in the Audio Output dialog (Ctrl+U) or from the Settings menu. Like the main device, it is remembered for each book, each podcast, and your music library.
- If one device sounds later than the other (Bluetooth headphones often do), set the second output delay in the same dialog to line them up by ear.

### Music Files & Tags
- Rename track files right from any music list with F2: the name becomes editable in place, just like in File Explorer. If a file with that name already exists, the player tells you and lets you choose another name. Your library, playlists, and playback queue keep the renamed track, even while it is playing.
- Edit a track's tags (title, artist, album, genre, year, lyrics, and more) with Ctrl+D.
- Edit the tags of a whole folder at once: press Ctrl+D on a folder to change the artist, album, genre, year, and other shared details of all its tracks, including those in its subfolders. Each tag can be kept as it is on every track, cleared, set to one of the values the tracks already have, or set to anything you type.
- If you close the player while tags are still being saved, it asks first, so no file is left half saved. If the sleep timer closes the player or shuts down the computer, it waits until the tags are saved.
- Press Space on a folder to hear the total duration of its music, and Shift+Space to hear how many tracks it holds and their size. Shift+Space on a track tells you its file size.
- Ctrl+C on a folder copies its path.
- The context menus of tracks and folders now include all of these actions.

### Music Transitions & Settings
- When you change tracks yourself, the next song now starts where its music actually begins, so manual switches no longer sound like a gap.
- New options for manual track changes: where the next song starts, how the switch sounds (crossfade, next song first, quick cut, or instant), and an option to shape the fade-in to the way the next song begins.
- Backspace in Music Mode can either go back to the beginning of the song or restart it using your manual switch settings.
- Music Playback Settings (Ctrl+Y) are now organized in two tabs: Automatic Transitions and Manual Track Change.
- Save your own Smart Radio presets, just like equalizer presets.

### Audiobooks & Bookmarks
- Shift+Space on a book tells you the size of its audio files, and Ctrl+C copies its location.
- In a book's track list and folder tree, Space tells you a track's duration, Shift+Space its size, and Ctrl+C copies its path.
- New context menus for books and their tracks, with options such as play, rename, rescan, copy path, and size.
- Pressing Enter on the track or chapter that is already playing now starts it again from the beginning.
- Press Ctrl+F in the Bookmark Manager to show all bookmarks, only those with notes, or only those without.

### Dialogs & Accessibility
- Settings such as the equalizer, sleep timer, playback fades, normalization, podcast playback, proxy, music playback, and rewind after pause, as well as bookmark notes, now open in their own dialogs that screen readers announce clearly and quickly.
- Folder and file pickers now open inside the main window.
- Smoother screen reader feedback: list and tree items are no longer read twice after filtering or deleting, and message boxes are announced sooner.
- After deleting or editing an item, focus returns to the right place, and emptied categories and playlists stay open so you can add items again.

### Bug Fixes
- Removing a book or podcast from inside a category now removes it everywhere right away.
- Setting the music folder while a playlist is playing no longer changes the playlist.
- Books you added manually inside your main audiobook folder are now merged into it, keeping their progress, bookmarks, and categories.
- Fixed the beginner guide hints and the window title when adding media.
- Fixed a Windows beep when opening the bookmark filter.
- General stability and performance improvements.

# Changes version 0.2.1

### Smart Mix & Audio Transitions
- Song endings are now recognized more accurately, keeping natural echoes, quiet lead-ins, and fading instruments intact.
- Echoes and trailing notes at the end of a song now fade out gently under the next song instead of being cut off.
- Added a "Tail handling" option (Fade or Cut) to the Custom mix settings.
- Classic crossfade with silence detection now starts smoothly where the music ends.

### Sound & Volume Stability
- Fixed rare cases where playback could cut out or go silent when using the equalizer, volume boost, or normalization.
- Volume normalization is now more reliable and keeps the volume steady.
- Songs with quiet intros now play at the right volume.

### Music Library & Performance
- Adding music folders manually no longer freezes the player while they are scanned.
- Scanning and sorting your library is now much faster.
- Screen reader feedback: pressing Ctrl+A while a folder is being scanned tells you the scan is in progress, and you hear when a longer scan is finished.

### Downloads
- Fixed the download queue getting stuck when a connection stalls or a server stops responding.
- Downloads that stall now reconnect and continue automatically from where they stopped.
- Cancelling a download now takes effect immediately, and the next download in the queue starts right away.
- The download queue now recovers reliably from errors.
- Downloads whose partial files were lost are now removed from the list automatically.

### General & Shortcuts
- Added Ctrl+F1 to open the user guide from anywhere in the player.

# Changes version 0.2

### Loudness Normalization
- Added loudness normalization, keeping the volume consistent across tracks and episodes.
- Separate loudness settings for Books, Podcasts, and Music.
- Added protection against distortion when boosting the volume above 100% or adjusting the equalizer, along with improved sound quality.
- Optional dynamic leveler with selectable strength (Light, Medium, Strong).
- Loudness is measured in the background and remembered, so playback starts without delay.
- Shortcuts: press Shift+B to turn loudness normalization on or off, or Ctrl+Shift+B to open its settings.

### Music Transitions & Smart Mixing
- Rebuilt track transitions that adapt to each song.
- Added transition presets: Standard, Energetic Radio, Gentle Flow, Club/DJ, Slam Cut, Quick Blend, Full Blend, Gap Close, and Custom.
- Fades stay accurate at any playback speed and when pausing.
- Silence between tracks is detected and removed automatically.
- Songs that begin abruptly with vocals or beats now come in naturally.
- Support for gapless album playback and adjustable fades when switching tracks yourself.

### Playback Fades & Smooth Seeking
- Added optional smooth fade-out and fade-in when pausing and resuming playback.
- Seeking within a track is now smooth, without clicks or jumps.
- The volume dips gently while you seek quickly or hold down the arrow keys.
- Separate fade settings for Book, Podcast, and Music playback (Ctrl+Shift+I).

### Podcast Downloads & Management
- Added "Download All Episodes": press Ctrl+S on a podcast to download all its episodes, from oldest to newest, or press Ctrl+S again to cancel the remaining downloads.
- Added Ctrl+Shift+S to cancel all pending and active downloads at once.
- Added "Show in Folder" (Ctrl+F) on downloaded episodes to find the file in Windows Explorer.
- Added Ctrl+Alt+J to open the downloads folder from anywhere in the player.
- Downloads pause automatically before your drive runs out of space.

### Accessibility & Screen Reader Improvements
- Fixed screen readers announcing "unavailable" when dialogs such as folder and file pickers open.

### Bug Fixes & Performance
- Fixed some podcast feeds, such as those hosted on Acast, that could not be added.
- Queuing or cancelling many downloads at once is now much faster.
- General performance and memory improvements.
