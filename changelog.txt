# Changes version 0.2.1

### Smart Mix & Audio Transitions
- Song endings are now recognized more accurately, keeping natural echoes, quiet lead-ins, and fading instruments intact.
- Echoes and trailing notes at the end of a song now fade out gently under the next song instead of being cut off.
- Added a "Tail handling" option (Fade or Cut) to the Custom mix settings.
- Classic crossfade with silence detection now starts smoothly where the music ends.

### Sound & Volume Stability
- Fixed rare cases where playback could cut out or go silent when using the equalizer, volume boost, or normalization.
- Volume normalization is now more reliable and keeps the volume steady.
- Songs with quiet intros now play at the right volume.

### Music Library & Performance
- Adding music folders manually no longer freezes the player while they are scanned.
- Scanning and sorting your library is now much faster.
- Screen reader feedback: pressing Ctrl+A while a folder is being scanned tells you the scan is in progress, and you hear when a longer scan is finished.

### Downloads
- Fixed the download queue getting stuck when a connection stalls or a server stops responding.
- Downloads that stall now reconnect and continue automatically from where they stopped.
- Cancelling a download now takes effect immediately, and the next download in the queue starts right away.
- The download queue now recovers reliably from errors.
- Downloads whose partial files were lost are now removed from the list automatically.

### General & Shortcuts
- Added Ctrl+F1 to open the user guide from anywhere in the player.

# Changes version 0.2

### Loudness Normalization
- Added loudness normalization, keeping the volume consistent across tracks and episodes.
- Separate loudness settings for Books, Podcasts, and Music.
- Added protection against distortion when boosting the volume above 100% or adjusting the equalizer, along with improved sound quality.
- Optional dynamic leveler with selectable strength (Light, Medium, Strong).
- Loudness is measured in the background and remembered, so playback starts without delay.
- Shortcuts: press Shift+B to turn loudness normalization on or off, or Ctrl+Shift+B to open its settings.

### Music Transitions & Smart Mixing
- Rebuilt track transitions that adapt to each song.
- Added transition presets: Standard, Energetic Radio, Gentle Flow, Club/DJ, Slam Cut, Quick Blend, Full Blend, Gap Close, and Custom.
- Fades stay accurate at any playback speed and when pausing.
- Silence between tracks is detected and removed automatically.
- Songs that begin abruptly with vocals or beats now come in naturally.
- Support for gapless album playback and adjustable fades when switching tracks yourself.

### Playback Fades & Smooth Seeking
- Added optional smooth fade-out and fade-in when pausing and resuming playback.
- Seeking within a track is now smooth, without clicks or jumps.
- The volume dips gently while you seek quickly or hold down the arrow keys.
- Separate fade settings for Book, Podcast, and Music playback (Ctrl+Shift+I).

### Podcast Downloads & Management
- Added "Download All Episodes": press Ctrl+S on a podcast to download all its episodes, from oldest to newest, or press Ctrl+S again to cancel the remaining downloads.
- Added Ctrl+Shift+S to cancel all pending and active downloads at once.
- Added "Show in Folder" (Ctrl+F) on downloaded episodes to find the file in Windows Explorer.
- Added Ctrl+Alt+J to open the downloads folder from anywhere in the player.
- Downloads pause automatically before your drive runs out of space.

### Accessibility & Screen Reader Improvements
- Fixed screen readers announcing "unavailable" when dialogs such as folder and file pickers open.

### Bug Fixes & Performance
- Fixed some podcast feeds, such as those hosted on Acast, that could not be added.
- Queuing or cancelling many downloads at once is now much faster.
- General performance and memory improvements.
