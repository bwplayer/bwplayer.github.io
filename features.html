Core Features

Blue Wave Player is built from the ground up to provide a seamless and accessible listening experience.

Designed for Accessibility

Built from the ground up for full screen reader compatibility with NVDA. Navigate the entire application using intuitive keyboard shortcuts with clear announcements and labels. Access elapsed, remaining, and total times for both current tracks and entire books at any time.

Four Dedicated Modes

Switch seamlessly between four specialized playback modes: Book Mode (F1) for local audiobooks, Podcast Mode (F2) for subscriptions and episode downloads, Music Mode (F3) for music collections and queues, and Explorer Mode (F4) for direct file and folder browsing with independent playback slots.

Independent Playback Engine

Book, Podcast, and Music Mode each operate an independent playback engine. Listen to a podcast while looking through your books, or play soft music in the background under an audiobook. Each mode can independently pause or keep playing when you switch modes.

Silence Removal & Shorten Pauses

Skip silence at the beginning and end of audiobook files with customizable sensitivity levels. Automatically shorten long pauses between sentences during playback for fluent listening without altering original file timing or bookmark positions. Skip repeated intro and outro audio across all tracks of a book.

Loudness Normalization

Built-in EBU R128 loudness normalization automatically balances audio levels so that older recordings, quiet speech, modern masters, and online radio streams play at a consistent perceived loudness without compressing dynamic range.

Internet Radio Streams

Stream live internet radio stations directly in Explorer Mode. The player displays real-time station names and current song titles, and supports custom stream lists.

Playlists & Playback Queue

Instantly create playlists from any track or folder. Manage a dedicated playback queue in Music Mode with queue duration announcements, and open multiple files together from Windows as temporary playlists.

10-Band Equalizer & Audio Profiles

Fine-tune audio with built-in presets tailored to spoken voice in Book and Podcast Modes, diverse presets for Music Mode, and customizable frequency adjustments remembered per book, podcast, or music collection.

Bookmarks and Notes

Place bookmarks at any position in audiobooks and podcasts, complete with optional text notes. Quickly browse, search, and jump to saved bookmarks.

Variable Speed & Pitch Control

Adjust playback speed and pitch to suit your preferred listening pace. The player accurately calculates remaining playback time based on the active playback speed.

Smart Sleep Timer

Set playback to stop after a specified number of minutes, at a designated clock time, or after the current track finishes. The sleep timer smoothly stops all active playback across all modes.

Rewind After Pause

Automatically rewinds a customizable few seconds when resuming playback after a pause or stop, helping you easily regain the context of what was playing.

Multi-Volume Audiobook Support

Seamlessly organize, track, and play complex audiobooks split across multiple parts, discs, or volumes with continuous listening progress.

Offline Podcast Downloads

Search and subscribe to RSS podcast feeds, view detailed episode show notes, and download episodes directly to your computer for offline listening.

Setup & Portable Flexibility

Choose between a standard Windows installation (per-user or system-wide) or a 100% portable copy for USB drives. Setup can automatically migrate libraries, bookmarks, and settings from existing portable copies.
